# Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Key Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Senior Leadership | Moderate–High | High | Business value, progress, major risks and decisions |
| HR Manager | High | Moderate | Business value, progress, HR requirements |
| IT Manager | High | High | Business value, technical progress, feasibility |
| Security | Moderate | High | Privacy, security controls, risk mitigation |
| Engineering Manager / Technical Lead | Moderate | High | Clear requirements, technical decisions, progress |
| HR/IT Knowledge Owners / SMEs | High | High | Accurate requirements, approved content, business value |
| Employees / Pilot Users | High | Low–Moderate | Reliable, accurate, easy-to-use tool |

## Stakeholder Considerations

Stakeholder influence may change throughout the project lifecycle.

Security has high influence because unresolved security or privacy concerns could prevent the MVP from proceeding to the pilot.

HR/IT Knowledge Owners and SMEs have high influence because they are responsible for validating the accuracy and approval of the knowledge used by the AI assistant.

Employees have high interest because they will directly use and evaluate the solution during the pilot.
