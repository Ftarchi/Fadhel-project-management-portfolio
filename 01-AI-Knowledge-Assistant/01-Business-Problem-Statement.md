# Business Problem Statement

Employees currently rely on multiple channels, including internal documentation, email, and direct support requests, to find answers to routine HR, IT, and Operations questions.

This makes it difficult and time-consuming for employees to locate accurate and consistent information. As a result, employee productivity is reduced while HR, IT, and Operations teams spend significant time responding to repetitive requests.

The current approach also creates a risk of inconsistent information being provided across different channels.

The organization therefore needs to identify a more efficient and scalable way to provide employees with timely, accurate, and consistent access to internal information.
