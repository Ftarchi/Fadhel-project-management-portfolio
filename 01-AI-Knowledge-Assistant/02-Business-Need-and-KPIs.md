# Business Need & Success Measures

## Business Need

The organization needs a more efficient and scalable way to provide employees with timely, accurate, and consistent answers to routine HR and IT questions while reducing the workload associated with repetitive support requests.

The project will measure whether the MVP creates measurable improvements in employee information access and support-team efficiency.

## Success Measures / KPIs

### 1. Average Time to Answer
- **Definition:** Average time from an employee submitting a question to receiving an acceptable answer.
- **Baseline:** To be established
- **Target:** To be established

### 2. Support Handling Time
- **Definition:** Average HR/IT time spent handling routine employee questions.
- **Baseline:** To be established
- **Target:** To be established

### 3. AI Response Accuracy
- **Definition:** Percentage of evaluated AI responses that correctly answer the employee's question and are supported by approved company information.
- **Baseline:** To be established during testing
- **Target:** ≥95% accurate responses*
- **Measurement:** Accuracy testing against a defined set of representative HR and IT questions.

*Target to be validated during the project.

### 4. Employee Satisfaction
- **Definition:** Employee satisfaction with the AI assistant experience.
- **Baseline:** To be established
- **Target:** To be established
- **Measurement:** Post-interaction employee survey.

### 5. AI Self-Service Resolution Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of eligible routine employee questions successfully resolved by the AI assistant without human intervention.
- **Baseline:** To be established
- **Target:** To be established
- **Measurement:** Percentage of eligible questions resolved successfully without escalation to HR or IT.
